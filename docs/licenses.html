License Compliance & Audit Overview

Rapid Download Manager is built on top of world-class, battle-tested open-source Rust libraries. Every direct and transitive dependency has been formally audited for licensing compatibility:

  • Permissive Licenses: 100% of dependencies use standard permissive licenses (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, Zlib, and Unicode-3.0).
  • Zero Viral Copyleft: The application contains zero GPL, AGPL, or non-commercial restricted libraries, ensuring 100% compliance with commercial and Microsoft Store distribution.
