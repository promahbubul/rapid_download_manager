# Microsoft Partner Center Submission Checklist

Follow this step-by-step checklist to submit Rapid Download Manager to the Microsoft Store:

## Step 1: Partner Center Setup
1. Log in to [partner.microsoft.com](https://partner.microsoft.com/).
2. Navigate to **Apps & Games** ➔ **New app submission**.
3. Reserve the app name: **Rapid Download Manager**.

## Step 2: Pricing and Availability
* **Price:** Free
* **Free trial:** None
* **Markets:** All countries and regions (Worldwide)
* **Visibility:** Public

## Step 3: Properties & Category
* **Category:** Utilities & tools
* **Subcategory:** File managers
* **Privacy Policy URL:** `https://github.com/promahbubul/rapid_download_manager/blob/main/store_listing/PRIVACY_POLICY.md`
* **Website:** `https://github.com/promahbubul/rapid_download_manager`
* **Support contact:** `https://github.com/promahbubul/rapid_download_manager/issues`

## Step 4: Age Ratings (IARC Questionnaire)
Answer the IARC questionnaire:
* Violence: No
* Sexual content: No
* Crude language: No
* Controlled substances: No
* Miscellaneous: Does the app share user location? No. Does the app allow users to interact? No.
* **Expected Result:** Rated for All Ages / Everyone (3+).

## Step 5: Packages
Upload the certified package:
* `dist\installer\RapidDownloadManager_v1.0.4.msix`
* Wait for automatic Cloud WACK (Windows App Certification Kit) validation to complete.

## Step 6: Store Listings (Upload Assets)
* **App Icon:** Upload `store_listing\assets\store_icon_512.png` and `store_icon_300.png`.
* **Screenshots:** Upload the 5 screenshots from `store_listing\assets\`:
  1. `screenshot_1_dashboard.png` (Main Dashboard)
  2. `screenshot_2_add_download.png` (Add Download Modal)
  3. `screenshot_3_browser_integration.png` (Browser Integration)
  4. `screenshot_4_scheduler.png` (Scheduler)
  5. `screenshot_5_about_updates.png` (About & Updates)
* **Hero Banner:** Upload `store_listing\assets\hero_banner_1920x1080.png`.
* **Descriptions & Features:** Copy and paste from `store_listing\STORE_LISTING.md`.

## Step 7: Review and Submit
* Review the submission summary.
* Click **Submit to the Store**.
* Approval typically takes 24–48 hours!
